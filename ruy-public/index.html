Ruy Mireles Law Firm is a pay-per-lead (PPL) client — his leads come out of the same shared Texas campaigns Walker runs, which also supply Bryan Rodriguez. Ad Spend is Ruy’s share: each day’s campaign spend ÷ every lead that campaign produced that day (Walker + Bryan + Ruy) × the leads sent to Ruy that day. Leads are matched to their campaign by email, phone, then name against Walker’s PPL delivery log. Rows marked TEST in Ruy’s sheet are left out everywhere, including the day’s lead count. Revenue: Ruy prepaid $25,000 for 50 leads at $500 per lead, regardless of status — revenue is $500 × leads delivered (up to 50) and Profit is revenue minus Ruy’s ad-spend share. Budget Left is $25,000 − $500 × every lead delivered so far. Status is read from the sheet’s Lead Status text (Signed / Rejected, otherwise Reviewing).
